LegalNurseD SI — Case Review Overview

Case: LNS-2026-001 — Resident Fall & Delayed Transfer

This demonstration packet organizes selected synthetic records into a source-linked clinical review. It does not determine negligence, causation, liability, damages, or legal conclusions.

Key chronology

May 12, 21:42: Resident documented as found on the floor reporting right hip discomfort. [Nursing Note p. 67]

May 13, 00:30: Pain 8/10 and inability to bear weight documented. [MAR/TAR p. 91]

May 13, 01:05: On-call provider notified; ED evaluation ordered. [Provider Log p. 103]

May 13, 03:14: Imaging documents right intertrochanteric fracture. [Radiology p. 142]

Unresolved questions

  • Why do the nursing note and incident report contain different fall times?
  • Were ordered post-fall neurologic checks completed but stored elsewhere?
  • What clinical changes occurred before provider notification?
  • What did the applicable facility policy require after an unwitnessed fall?

Professional boundary

LegalNurseD SI is decision support for qualified reviewers. Final opinions, legal strategy, expert testimony, and case conclusions require appropriate human professionals.
